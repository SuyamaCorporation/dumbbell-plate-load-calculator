# Dumbbell & Barbell Plate Load Calculator

Enter your target barbell weight and available plate sizes to instantly compute the minimum plate combination needed per side, including bar weight, with a color-coded visual bar diagram showing the loading. Supports both kg and lb, and lets you toggle which plate denominations you own.

Two additional tabs provide a 1-rep max estimator using the Epley formula (with a full percentage breakdown table) and a warmup progression planner that auto-generates six warmup sets from bar-only through your working weight.
